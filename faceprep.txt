FACE Prep is an industry leader in placement training and technical skill development.
The primary programs offered by FACE Prep include Campus Recruitment Training (CRT) and Pro-Grad programs.
FACE Prep has trained over 3 million students across more than 1500 colleges in India.
The company partners with major IT service companies to provide corporate training and onboarding solutions.
The Pro-Grad program focuses on full-stack development, data science, and advanced programming skills.
FACE Prep's head office is located in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.
